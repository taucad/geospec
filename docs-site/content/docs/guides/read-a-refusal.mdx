---
title: Read a refusal
description: Tell a refused claim from a failed one, and decide what to change.
---

A claim has three outcomes. **Passed** and **failed** are answers about the geometry. **Refused** means the engine could not decide the claim on the geometry it was given, and says why with a typed diagnostic.

A refusal is never a pass. Negating a claim does not turn a refusal into a pass either: unsupported or insufficient evidence refuses under either polarity.

## Find the reason

A refused result has a `status` of `refused` and at least one diagnostic. The code names the reason; the message names the claim and what it needed:

```json
{
  "claimId": "hole",
  "status": "refused",
  "diagnostics": [
    {
      "code": "GEOSPEC_EVIDENCE_UNSUPPORTED",
      "message": "expectGeo(...).toHaveCircularHole() needs exact BRep circular-hole evidence, which this subject does not carry."
    }
  ]
}
```

## Decide what to change

- **The evidence is missing.** Feature claims such as circular holes need BRep evidence. A triangle mesh does not carry it, so admit the STEP export of the same part.
- **The claim is outside the admitted domain.** Registry membership does not mean every subject or profile supports a query. Ask the engine what it admits with an `initialize` request; its response lists each capability with its implementation and qualification state.
- **The spec is asking the wrong question.** If the mesh is all you have, claim what a mesh can answer, such as its bounds or whether it is watertight.
- **The subject is too large for a limit.** `GEOSPEC_ANALYSIS_LIMIT` means the claim needs more analysis memory than the engine's analysis limit allows, and `GEOSPEC_RESOURCE_LIMIT` means derived evidence exceeds an admission limit such as the triangle or occurrence count. The message states what the claim needed and the limit; `details` carries `limit`, `required` and `unit`, and for exact mesh scratch also `reservation`, `positions` and `triangles`. Admit a coarser or smaller subject, or a region of it, and keep the claim.
- **The claim ran out of work units.** `MATCHER_TIMEOUT` counts work units, not time: `details` carries the `budget` and `unitsUsed`. Raise the work-unit budget or narrow the claim.

Never change a test to accept a refusal as a pass. A suite that passes on refusals passes on geometry nobody measured.
