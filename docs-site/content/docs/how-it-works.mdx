description: Subjects, claims, canonical plans and results, and what the engine promises about each.

## The pieces

A **subject** is one model the engine has admitted: a mesh in `mesh-buffer-v1`, or a STEP or glTF/GLB file with its declared coordinate frame and resource identities. The engine names each subject by the hash of its bytes.

A **claim** states one property of one or more subjects. It names a capability from the engine's registry, a polarity (positive or negated), a work budget and a payload with the expected values.

A **plan** binds ordered subjects, claims, polarity, budgets and the numerical profile. The engine produces the canonical plan for a request, then evaluates it.

A **result** reports each claim as passed, failed or refused, with the evidence measured and any diagnostics.

## The engine

The engine is a single-threaded Rust core with an OCCT bridge for BRep and the Rust Manifold backend. It ships three ways from one source: a Node native addon, one combined Rust and OCCT WASM module for Node and browsers, and a CPython 3.13 and 3.14 extension.

Hosts send ordinary `ArrayBuffer`-backed `Uint8Array`s (bytes in Python). Rust owns validation, canonical plans, measurements and result serialization. The control contract is protocol 3, registry 5 and the `geospec-jcs-v1` canonical profile.

## What a result promises

**Refusal over guessing.** Unsupported or insufficient evidence produces an explicit refusal rather than a geometric pass, including under negation. Registry membership alone does not mean every subject or profile supports a query; the engine's initialization response lists the capabilities and the domains it admits.

**Reproducible bytes.** Canonical JSON uses UTF-16 key ordering and finite binary64 rendering. Exact byte equality holds within one engine profile, not between different numerical backends.

**Declared tolerances.** Approximate measurements use each matcher's declared tolerance and threshold semantics. Floating-point geometry does not promise a mathematically exact scalar. Equality is `abs(measured - expected) <= tolerance`; zero tolerance means nominal equality.

## Meshes and glTF

`mesh-buffer-v1` is ASCII `GSM1`, little-endian u32 vertex and triangle counts, then f64 coordinates and u32 triangle indices. In glTF, all TRIANGLES primitives of a mesh carrying `EXT_mesh_manifold` over its declared POSITION accessor form one material partition; separate meshes, instancing nodes and undeclared primitives stay separate operands.

A content hash identifies bytes. It does not establish that the geometry is correct: coordinates, units and transforms must come through the ingestion request.
