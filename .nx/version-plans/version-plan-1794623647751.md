`GeoSpecFactProvenanceMethod` gains `'bracket'`, the provenance of a bounding box the facts layer brackets under numeric profile `geospec-demand-v7`, which stays declared and refused until it ships. Under the served `geospec-demand-v6`, `toHaveBoundingBox` is unchanged: it reports the `AddOptimal` box with its legacy record, and no bracket is computed.

Under `geospec-demand-v7`, a STEP subject whose every solid the facts layer boxes to its target, a bracket no wider than 1e-7 of the solid's largest box side, reports each side's midpoint between a value a point of the subject attains and a value no point of its definition exceeds, with `bracket` provenance and a bound, and the interval rule decides its claims.

The bracket bounds the definition, not the kernel's geometry, which can lie outside it by up to `fileTolerance`, plus the gap bound on a solid with a bridged tolerance gap; `fileTolerance` excludes that gap bound, and only `witnesses.bracket.outerTolerant` covers it. Measured sides differ from the `AddOptimal` ones, usually in the last digits and by up to 0.56 model units on the measured corpus. A larger shift is not always `AddOptimal`'s error: of the 19 measured files where `AddOptimal` lies beyond the outer side by more than the bracket's target, OCCT's own sampled geometry lies past it by as much on 4, within `fileTolerance`.

A claim whose tolerance the bound straddles refuses with `GEOSPEC_ACCURACY_INSUFFICIENT`, and so does a comparator claim whose limit lies within the bound of the measured value, a limit equal to it included.

The evidence adds `witnesses.bracket`, with the inner and outer boxes and `outerTolerant`. A subject with geometry outside its solids, a solid the facts layer refuses, or a solid whose bracket stays wider than its target (`bracket-too-wide`, with the width or, when refinement stopped early, a lower bound on it) keeps the `AddOptimal` box and its legacy record and reports why in `witnesses.bracketRefusal`; a refused bracket is never stored. Solids are boxed in order and the first that refuses stops the bracket.
