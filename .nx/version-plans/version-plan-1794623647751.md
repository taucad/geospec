A `toHaveBoundingBox` claim on a STEP subject now reports the facts layer's bracket box when the layer boxes every solid. Each side is the midpoint between a value a point of the subject attains and a value no point exceeds, so measured boxes differ from the earlier `AddOptimal` boxes, usually in the last digits. Where `AddOptimal` was loose or short, or the bracket stays wide, a side can move by more than a model unit; the bound reports the bracket's width. The accuracy record reports `bracket` provenance with a bound, so the interval rule decides these claims: one whose tolerance the bound straddles refuses with `GEOSPEC_ACCURACY_INSUFFICIENT`.

The evidence adds `witnesses.bracket`, with the inner and outer boxes and `outerTolerant`. A subject with geometry outside its solids, or a solid the facts layer refuses, keeps the `AddOptimal` box and reports why in `witnesses.bracketRefusal`. `GeoSpecFactProvenanceMethod` gains `'bracket'`.
