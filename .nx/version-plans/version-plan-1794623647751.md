A `toHaveBoundingBox` claim on a STEP subject now reports the facts layer's bracket box when the layer boxes every solid to its target, a bracket no wider than 1e-7 of the solid's largest box side. Each side is the midpoint between a value a point of the subject attains and a value no point exceeds, so measured boxes differ from the earlier `AddOptimal` boxes, usually in the last digits. Where `AddOptimal` fell short of the subject's geometry or lay beyond it, a side moves by more than the bracket's width, by up to 0.56 model units on the measured corpus.

The accuracy record reports `bracket` provenance with a bound, so the interval rule decides these claims: one whose tolerance the bound straddles refuses with `GEOSPEC_ACCURACY_INSUFFICIENT`.

The evidence adds `witnesses.bracket`, with the inner and outer boxes and `outerTolerant`. A subject with geometry outside its solids, a solid the facts layer refuses, or a solid whose bracket stays wider than its target (`bracket-too-wide`, with the width or, when refinement stopped early, a lower bound on it) keeps the `AddOptimal` box and its legacy record and reports why in `witnesses.bracketRefusal`; a refused bracket is never stored. Solids are boxed in order and the first that refuses stops the bracket. `GeoSpecFactProvenanceMethod` gains `'bracket'`.
