The facts integrator checks every outer panel on a NURBS face against the face's normal over the region its inner integrals cover. It builds each knot-span cell's homogeneous Bézier net of the normal and subdivides it with de Casteljau. A panel is resolved only where the net's coefficients stay within 60 degrees of one direction along the region's base and trim lines and along the v knot lines between them, and, over every v strip of the rest, either do the same or turn monotonically along v. An unresolved panel counts its whole absolute integral as its error, so it splits while the face's summed error is over the tolerance. This covers rational nets, curved trims and outer directions that cancel, where the outer panels had no such check. On cubic probes, two close kinks along a curved trim gave 22,496 times the area bound and now stay within it. So does `z = f(u) + H v^5`, where the outer hull's 1.49 times came from bounding the v integral of the normal rather than its length.

A cell whose normal net is not finite, or a trim that cannot be bounded over a panel, refuses `non-finite`. A face whose summed error never fits within the panel cap refuses `no-convergence`, as before, and so does a face whose normal nets would hold more than 2^21 coefficients or take more than 2^34 operations to check.
