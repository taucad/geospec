The facts integrator checks every outer panel on a NURBS face against the face's normal over the region its inner integrals cover. It builds each knot-span cell's homogeneous Bézier net of the normal and subdivides it with de Casteljau. A panel passes only where the net's coefficients stay within 60 degrees of one direction along the region's base and trim lines, and, over every v strip of the rest, either do the same or turn monotonically along v. Otherwise the panel splits. This covers rational nets, curved trims and outer directions that cancel, where the outer panels had no such check. On cubic probes, two close kinks along a curved trim gave 22,496 times the area bound and now stay within it. So does `z = f(u) + H v^5`, where the outer hull's 1.49 times came from bounding the v integral of the normal rather than its length.

A cell whose normal net is not finite, or a trim that cannot be bounded over a panel, refuses `non-finite`. A face that needs more splits than the panel cap refuses `no-convergence`, as before.
