Under numeric profile `geospec-demand-v7`, which stays declared and refused until it ships, a STEP subject's bounded volume, surface area, mass and centre of mass no longer measure OCCT's `BRepGProp` beside the facts layer, and `witnesses.shadow` is gone. No verdict read it, and it cost about one more mass integration per subject.

A measured subject's bounded mass facts are kept in the facts store as `boundedMass`, keyed on `geospec-demand-v7`; a refusal is never stored. Each claim that reads them is charged one work unit per integrated face plus its integration panels, the same at every thread count and whether the facts were integrated, retained or stored. Under the served `geospec-demand-v6` nothing changes, byte for byte.
