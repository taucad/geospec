The exact bounding box now covers more faces whose trims leave a B-spline net's domain. With the kernel's tolerance given, a trim may leave the domain by up to 1e-3 of its width, where the limit was 1e-6, on a face whose kernel extrapolates the net. The face refuses `trim-outside-domain` when the extrapolation widening exceeds the body's tolerance or the extrapolated weights cannot be shown positive.

Without the kernel's tolerance, and on a face whose kernel evaluates a periodic net, the limit stays 1e-6. An extrapolated face now refines its sides only to the larger of the target width and the widening.
