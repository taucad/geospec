The exact bounding box now covers more faces whose trims leave their surface's domain. With the kernel's tolerance given, a trim may leave a B-spline net's domain, or pass a sphere's pole, by up to 1e-3 of the domain's width, where the limit was 1e-6, on a face whose kernel extrapolates the surface. Such a face refuses `trim-outside-domain` when the extrapolation widening exceeds the body's tolerance or the extrapolated weights cannot be shown positive, and its sides refine only to the larger of the target width and the widening.

A face whose trims stay within 1e-6 of the domain gets the same box as before, with or without the kernel's tolerance. Without the tolerance, and on a face whose kernel evaluates a periodic net, the limit stays 1e-6.
