Report open edges shared across parts in `toBeWatertight`. Its evidence gains `openEdgesSharedAcrossLabels`, the welded edges left open by two or more parts, and `sharedOpenEdgeLabels`, those parts' base labels, in both evidence profiles. When open edges coincide across parts, the failure suggestion names the other part and `EXT_mesh_manifold`. Verdicts, edge counts, clusters, per-primitive rows and refusals are unchanged.

The READMEs, the reference and guide pages and the authoring doctrine now state the glTF unit: a node with a mesh is a part, its `TRIANGLES` primitives are that part's surface, separately named nodes are separate parts, and `EXT_mesh_manifold` makes a mesh's primitives one body.
