The facts integrator splits more panels where a face's normal passes close to zero. Its length then has a softened kink that a panel's error estimate can miss: on cubic nets, a kink beside a bisection point gave 833 times the area bound, and two close kinks gave 24,823 times. Directions are now compared between neighbouring nodes and next to every panel's ends. Outer integrals on planes, cylinders, spheres and tori whose major radius is at least the minor radius skip these checks.

On NURBS faces, each inner panel's Bernstein hull of the normal must also keep its direction within 60 degrees. So must each outer panel's on non-rational nets along straight trims, where every node has a direction. Elsewhere, such as along curved trims, two close kinks between nodes can still be missed.
