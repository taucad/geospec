The facts integrator's area bound now contains the error on steep B-spline nets. Where a face's normal passes close to zero, its length has a softened kink that the integration panels could straddle, and the area's error estimate could miss it: on a cubic net with poles 3,000 units high the area was off by 13.6 times its bound. A panel is now split until the normal's direction turns by no more than about half a radian between neighbouring nodes, including across the gap between a knot span's end and its nearest node. Faces whose normals turn slowly keep the same facts, and the volume and centre of mass are unaffected except through the extra panels.
