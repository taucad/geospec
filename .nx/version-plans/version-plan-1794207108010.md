The exact bounding box now contains the surface on faces whose trims run slightly past a B-spline net's domain. It bounds the net's extrapolation by the blossomed hull of the end span, and at a corner by the hull of both end spans. Before, it used a Lipschitz bound, which could be too small next to a narrow end span or a small end weight. A net whose extrapolated weights cannot be shown positive refuses with `trim-outside-domain`. Faces inside their domain keep the same box bit for bit.
