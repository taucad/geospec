Speed up cold STEP admission. On native builds, OCCT's own allocation entry points (`Standard::Allocate`, `AllocateOptimal`, `Reallocate` and `Free`) now use a statically linked mimalloc pinned through the `libmimalloc-sys` crate, which shortens shape repair and reader teardown; `malloc` itself is not replaced and Rust and Node allocations are unchanged. Under Node, the WASM build digests large subject bytes with `node:crypto` through the engine's own glue, and falls back to its built-in SHA-256 in browsers. Subject hashes, admitted shapes, reports and verdicts are unchanged.
