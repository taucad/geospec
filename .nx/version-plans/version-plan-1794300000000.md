The exact bounding box now covers solids whose faces have tolerance gaps: edges that meet at a vertex within its tolerance while their 2D curves do not. The facts still refuse such a face with `tolerance-gap`. The box takes the face with each gap closed by its straight join, and ties every join to its vertex: a join outside the vertex's tolerance, without a vertex or without a bound refuses `tolerance-gap`, and an open join refuses `open-trim-loop`.

A join across the period of a cylinder or a cone stands for its one image within the vertex's tolerance. The box reports its gap faces, its gap bound and its wrapped joins, and its tolerant box grows by the gap bound. Other boxes are unchanged bit for bit.
