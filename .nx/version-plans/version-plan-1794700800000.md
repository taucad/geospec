Under numeric profile `geospec-demand-v7`, which stays declared and refused until it ships, the volume, surface area, mass and centre of mass of a STEP subject come from the facts layer with a bound. Each fact reports `exact-by-degree` or `adaptive` provenance, and the interval rule decides its claims. Under the served `geospec-demand-v6` these claims are unchanged, byte for byte, and no facts are integrated.

`fileTolerance` is the solids' largest tolerance. Volume and mass carry it times each solid's area as their gap; the centre of mass carries how far that gap volume can move it, given the solids' reach from the centre. A claim tolerance below the gap, or a comparator whose limit lies within the bound plus the gap of the measured value, refuses with `GEOSPEC_FILE_TOLERANCE_EXCEEDS_CLAIM`.

A solid with an open shell refuses with `GEOSPEC_SUBJECT_NOT_CLOSED`. A subject the facts layer refuses, or a shape `BRepCheck` finds invalid, refuses with `GEOSPEC_ACCURACY_INSUFFICIENT`, a null bound and `details.factsRefusal`; no other measurement stands in.
