Thread native STEP admission. On native builds, a STEP transfer now completes the translation of independent manifold solids on at most two worker threads while it goes on reading, and releases the STEP reader on a background thread after admission returns. A solid that shares curves or topology with a solid still being completed, and every other kind of shape, is completed on the calling thread; a threaded transfer that cannot reproduce the serial result is read again serially. Set `GEOSPEC_SERIAL_STEP_ADMISSION=1` to keep all admission work on the calling thread. The WASM build and claim evaluation stay single-threaded. Subject hashes, admitted shapes, reports, verdicts, refusals and work charges are unchanged.
