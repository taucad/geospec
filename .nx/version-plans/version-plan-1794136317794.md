The BRep validity check classifies fewer wire pairs on faces with many holes. The private OCCT build's `BRepCheck_Face` now gives a wire a UV box when one of its edges runs past the period of an untrimmed circle, ellipse or periodic B-spline trim, boxing that edge by the whole closed curve, so hole wires whose boxes are disjoint skip pairwise classification as other wires already did. Validity facts, their reasons, verdicts and refusals are unchanged.
