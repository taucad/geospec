Decide concave cylinder walls that span at most half a turn and meet other surfaces at both axial edges, such as rounded cutout and pocket corners, slot ends, flutes and gear tooth roots. They report `open-arc` instead of refusing as `incomplete-band`, so a part without the claimed hole fails rather than refuses. A slit bore, whose wall spans more than half a turn, still refuses.
