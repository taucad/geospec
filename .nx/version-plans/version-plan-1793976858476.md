Persist completed facts between runs. A native engine with a cache root stores validity, exact shell closure, the bounding box, volume with centre of mass, surface area and topology counts in the `geospec-facts-v1` family, one write-once entry per subject and fact, and reads them back instead of measuring again. `geospec run`, `setupGeoSpecVitestProject` and the pytest plugin open `$XDG_CACHE_HOME/geospec/<engine version>` by default; `GEOSPEC_FACTS_STORE` names another absolute directory, or `off`. Results are byte-identical with and without the store.

Verdicts, refusals and failures are never stored, and a stored closure charges the work units its measurement charged. The store moves to a sharded layout with a ledger, reads take no lock, and the facts family has its own cap of 65,536 entries; entries in the earlier layout are left in place and miss. Observations add `facts.storeHits` and `facts.storeWrites`.
