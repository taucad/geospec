Qualify circular bores whose rim is not a planar circle, such as a cross hole through a pin, a set-screw hole through a collar's wall or a hole through a sloped face. Such a rim ends in a new `oblique-mouth` termination when the face across leaves the whole rim away from the bore's axis at an angle. A curved floor that closes the bore, a hole tangent to the wall and material inside the cylinder beyond an oblique rim still refuse.
