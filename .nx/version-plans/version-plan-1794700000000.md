The facts integrator now bounds three round-off terms that it left out of both the value and the bound. Each one gave a face's area outside its bound against an independent quadrature of the exported definition in the 415-file set.

A joint whose ends differ by at most 64 unit round-offs, and a trim interval too short to step that moves that little, now add their closing integral `-F du` to the bound. That integral grows with `F`, up to the body's size squared, and not with the face's area. A face of Feather RP2040 USB Case was 1.5e-10 from its closed loop against a bound of 2e-15.

A joint whose computed ends meet may still be open in the definition by the rounding of those ends. Each joint now adds the integral over that width as well. On Pico Case, a plane whose parameters sit near u = -5e5 was 2.4e-12 off against a bound of 9.6e-15.

The crossings of a trim with the surface's u knot lines are now bisected until the bracket holds adjacent doubles, not to 1e-12. Across a hard line the integrand jumps, and a break a few 1e-12 short of the line left the jump inside a panel. On blackpants a face was 6.4e-15 off against a bound of 2.5e-15.

Bounds grow by these terms. Values change only by round-off, on faces whose trims cross a u knot line between the crossing search's samples. Faces whose integrals were exact stay exact.
