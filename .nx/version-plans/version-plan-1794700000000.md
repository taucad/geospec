The facts integrator now bounds round-off terms that it left out of both the value and the bound. Each one gave a face's area outside its bound against an independent quadrature of the exported definition in the 415-file set, or in a constructed face.

A joint whose ends differ by at most 64 unit round-offs, and a trim interval too short to step that moves that little, now add their closing integral `-F du` to the bound, taken along the joint's own chord. That integral grows with `F`, up to the body's size squared, and not with the face's area. A face of Feather RP2040 USB Case was 1.5e-10 from its closed loop against a bound of 2e-15.

A joint whose computed ends meet may still be open in the definition by the rounding of those ends. Each joint now adds, around each piece's own computed end, that reach times the largest `|F|` across it, split at every hard u knot line inside the reach and held within the surface's domain. On Pico Case, a plane whose parameters sit near u = -5e5 was 2.4e-12 off against a bound of 9.6e-15. A piece's end rounding comes from the curve's span at that end, so a tiny weight elsewhere no longer makes it unbounded.

A trim's crossings of the surface's u knot lines are now bisected until the bracket holds adjacent doubles. Across a hard line the integrand jumps, and the crossing's u is known only to its rounding: each hard crossing adds the same reach to the bound. On blackpants a face was 6.4e-15 off against a bound of 2.5e-15.

Bounds grow by these terms and shrink where panels no longer straddle a knot line. In the 415-file set no body changes between served and refused, values move by at most 0.03 of the new bound, and integration takes about 4% longer.
