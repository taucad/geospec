The facts integrator now bounds joints whose ends differ by round-off only. Before, a joint apart by at most 64 unit round-offs, and a trim interval too short to step that moves that little, counted in neither the value nor the bound. The closing integral `-F du` across such a joint grows with `F`, up to the body's size squared, and not with the face's area.

On thin faces far from the base of the inner integrals, the omitted term exceeded the bound. In the 415-file set, a face of Feather RP2040 USB Case was 1.5e-10 from its closed loop against a bound of 2e-15. Both terms now go into the face's bound like a gap or a chord. The face stays exact, and values do not change.
